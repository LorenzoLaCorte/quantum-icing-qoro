# Method

slot-QLNS (classical): large-neighbourhood search that removes up to 6 units of one period and re-chooses 6 log-encoded slots jointly from 300 random proposals per move, each polished by exact single/pair unit descent and accepted on the exact multi-period objective (1,500 s, started from our previous best .sol; code: portfolio_QIcing/formulation/slot_qlns.py).
